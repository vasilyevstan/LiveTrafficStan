Transformation:
  Each group was validated independently. GP and SATCAT rows were joined by
  canonical decimal NORAD catalog ID. Six non-conflicting cross-group
  overlaps were deduplicated by that ID, with the newest valid OMM epoch
  retained. Records are canonically sorted by numeric NORAD ID. sourceGroups
  retain reviewed order, and displayOrder is derived only from the first
  reviewed source-group position and numeric NORAD ID.

Meaning:
  The file contains orbital elements and catalog metadata, not live telemetry.
  Group membership is not object purpose, operational state, or optical
  visibility. Any positions displayed by LiveTrafficStan are modeled locally
  and do not prove naked-eye visibility, powered flight, reentry, impact,
  conjunction, or hazard.
